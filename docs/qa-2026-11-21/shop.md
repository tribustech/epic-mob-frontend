## What works
- **All 52 product pages** render with HTTP 200, a correct h1, title and breadcrumb, and no JS page errors. The only console 404 is `/_vercel/insights/script.js`, which is expected off Vercel.
- **All 281 colour swatches** switch the photo (always `/<slug>/<colour>.webp`, loaded, naturalWidth > 0), the price, the legend, `aria-pressed` and the `?culoare=` URL. Deep links `?culoare=` open the right colour. Swatch price, main price and the „prețul obișnuit” note always agree.
- **No broken images.** Every card picture belongs to its own product, and card prices equal the product page price for the same colour (52/52).
- **Filters:** every option's count badge equals the number of products shown, across 18 single filters and 112 combinations. Options with 0 results are disabled, empty combinations show a clear empty state with „Șterge filtrele” and the Atelier link, chips remove single filters, and „Șterge filtrele” resets to 52. Filters live in the URL and survive Back with the scroll position. „Toate ofertele” filters in place (6 products) and scrolls to the list.
- **Sorting** by price, ascending and descending, is correctly ordered. „Recomandate” puts offers first.
- **Phone filter sheet** (native dialog): opens, has a live „Arată N produse” button, and shows a badge with the active filter count. No horizontal scroll at 390 or 430 on the list, product or cart pages.
- **Mobile product page:** the sticky price and „Adaugă în coș” bar appears when the main button scrolls away, and the floating cart button hides under it. The 3D view switch loads a canvas.
- **„Personalizează”** opens /configurator with the exact model and colour for all 52 products (URL ends in `#oferta`, no errors). For non-offer products the price matches the shop.
- **Cart:** add, adding again merges into the same line, +/− (− disabled at 1, + disabled at 5), remove, empty state, the 8-line limit with a clear „Coșul e plin…” message, a live floating cart count, and the total equals the sum of the line totals.
- **Order form:** errors in Romanian appear under each field, the first bad field gets focus, errors clear as the field is fixed, and whitespace-only input is rejected. A valid order went to the mock with HTTP 200 in about 1 s. The success state appears, the cart and floating button are cleared, and the page scrolls to the top. The honeypot field is hidden from people and screen readers.
- Romanian copy uses correct comma-below ș/ț throughout. No cedilla characters were found in the shop code.
- axe: the product page and the cart have no violations.
